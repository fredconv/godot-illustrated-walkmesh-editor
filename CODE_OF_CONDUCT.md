# Code of Conduct

IWE is developed in the open and welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information without consent, and any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this standard, and may restrict participation when needed.

To report a concern, open a GitHub issue marked as a conduct concern, or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile of the repository owner. Reports are handled confidentially.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
